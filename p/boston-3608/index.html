Willow Baker Development

191 homes · Under construction · Dorchester, Boston.

The proposed project consists of the demolition of the existing structures occupying parcels located along Willow, Baker, and Fields Court and the construction of 3 buildings, totaling 181,398 square feet and would include approximately 191 residential units, 46 garage and on-street parking spaces, and associated public realm and open space improvements.
