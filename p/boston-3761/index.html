40 Soldiers Field Place

61 homes · Complete · Brighton, Boston.

The Proponent envisions a residential transformation of an underutilized commercial property site at the northern edge of the Brighton neighborhood. In particular, the proposed project would raze and replace an outdated commercial building with a new six story (6) building of approximately 67,835 gross square feet, including 61 residential units, on-site parking garage for 49 vehicles and bicycle storage, new open space, public realm, pedestrian connectivity upgrades and overall site improvements.
