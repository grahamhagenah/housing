Old Colony Phase 6

89 homes · Complete · South Boston, Boston.

Beacon Communities is proposing a Notice of Project Change to the original Old Colony Master Plan Development. The Project is also seeking to use the 121A tool for development. Phase Six will be comprised of approximately 89 apartments with 1-5 bedrooms for individuals, families and seniors. In addition, it will include approximately 6,662 gross square feet of accessory community space that will be available for lease to local community service organizations that improve the quality of life for community residents. It is anticipated that the space will initially be leased by a local South Boston arts organization that works with low-income individuals.
