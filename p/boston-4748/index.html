77 Terrace Street

48 homes · Under construction · Mission Hill, Boston.

48 units of affordable ownership to be constructed on City of Boston land following tentative designation by City/MOH. The Project will create 48 units of ownership housing (80% and 100% of Boston AMI) along with approximately 2,000 sf of ground-floor retail with garage parking. The building is located between Parker Street and Terrace Street in the Mission Hill neighborhood of Boston.
