84 Stonley Road

21 homes · Proposed · Jamaica Plain, Boston.

The project proposes construction of a 5-story building with 28 rental units (5 of which will be affordable). The building will fill the lot along Stonley Road and Stedman Street, defining the existing street wall and creating a gateway between the two streets. The building is within walking distance of both MBTA Orange Line Forest Hills and Green Street stations, as well as MBTA bus routes along Washington Street. The project proposes no parking.
