1201 River Street

63 homes · Complete · Hyde Park, Boston.

The Proposed Project at 1201 River Street includes a 4-story, approximately 49,900 gross square foot, 63-unit all-affordable apartment community for seniors and older adults ages 55 and up with approximately 1,202 square feet of ground floor commercial space, a fitness center, community room, library, laundry center, mail and package room, management office, 32 bike parking spaces and up to 40 vehicular parking spaces. The development also includes an outdoor courtyard with a grilling and seating area, a bocce court, benches, a yard, and landscaping.The development will be an example of efficiency and sustainability achieving DND Zero Emission Buildings requirements as a non-carbon producing all-electric building constructed in accordance with Passive House Certified standards.
