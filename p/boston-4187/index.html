26-28 Coffey Street

24 homes · Approved · Dorchester, Boston.

Construction of a new 4-story building with a common roof deck that will include twenty-four (24) total Dwelling Units, twelve (12) of which are two-bedroom residential units , six (6) one-bedroom residential units and six (6) studio units, an Activity Room with a dedicated entrance from the courtyard, a lobby/sitting area, plus a bicycle storage and repair area off the common lobby. Twenty-seven (75) vehicle parking spaces accessed via a new curb cut on Coffee Street. The two (2) existing curb cuts will be extinguished.
