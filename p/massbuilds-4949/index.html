571 Revere Street

51 homes · Complete · Revere.

571 REVERE ST is a transit-oriented, new construction development that will create 51 new rental homes for families (all will be affordable below 120% AMI or lower). The site is a short walk to Revere Beach and “Waterfront Square,” where the City of Revere has encouraged private investment and economic development through the creation of more than 700 market-rate rental apartments and a 170-suite hotel. 571 Revere will complement this investment by expanding housing options for households with a range of incomes
