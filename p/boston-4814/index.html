65-79 Seattle Street

43 homes · Approved · Allston, Boston.

A mixed-income development with forty-three (43) homeownership units of which twenty-two (22) of the units will be restricted to households earning at or below 80% AMI, and twenty-one (21) of the units will be restricted to households earning at or below 100% AMI. The project is broken up into three separate buildings and capped at three (3) stories. The project provides 15,000 sf of open space, and an internal walkway underneath that connects the site to the future greenway.
