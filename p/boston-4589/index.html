90 Allandale

8 homes · Approved · West Roxbury, Boston.

The 90 Allandale proposal seeks approval to construct on an 87,162 square-foot lot. The lot is currently occupied by a single-family dwelling, which would be razed and replaced with the proposed development. The Proposal seeks to construct a new four (4) phase residential development comprised of four (4) two and a half story townhouses each containing two (2) single-family dwelling units, for a total of eight (8) home ownership condominiums on the northerly portion of the lot. Each condominium is proposed to be two and a half stories and contain three (3) bedrooms. The southerly portion of the lot is proposed to be protected from future development with the recording of a deed restriction. The proponent seeks to include two garage parking spaces per unit.
