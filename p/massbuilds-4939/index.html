Clarendon Hill

409 homes · Proposed · Hillside, Somerville.

Proposed replacement and redevelopment of two-hundred and sixteen (216) state public housing units. In its place, 591 rental units in four (4) buildings. In addition, thirty-four (34) town-homes. All current public housing residents will have the opportunity to return. Total of 369 parking spaces. Two-phase development. Also aims to provide elderly housing.
