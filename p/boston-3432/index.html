135 Dudley Street

170 homes · Approved · Roxbury, Boston.

The 135 Dudley Street mixed-use residential/commercial project is adjacent to the B-2 Police Station, the Roxbury Municipal Court and the Dudley Literacy Center, with a total of 282,382 gross square feet (gsf) of floor area in two buildings of nine and four floors including 168 multi-family units (108 condos and 60 rentals); 8,703 gsf of commercial space; and 146 garage parking spaces including 25 spaces open to the public.
