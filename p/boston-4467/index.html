Bartlett Station | Lot C

61 homes · Approved · Roxbury, Boston.

The proposed development at Bartlett Station (also known as, "Bartlett Place Lot C ") will construct a 4-story building containing 61 mixed-income homeownership units, of which 12 are affordable and 4 are dedicated to Artist Live/Work. The building will also feature a variety of residential amenities, including bike parking, lounge and gallery space for artist use, a dog washing room, mail room, private inner courtyard, and a below-grade parking garage with 51 spaces.
