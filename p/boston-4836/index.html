350 West Fourth Street

35 homes · Approved · South Boston, Boston.

Redevelopment of a 43,996 square-foot Project Site which is currently occupied by an accessory surface parking to the adjacent Bigelow Condominium Building (“Bigelow”). The proposed multi-family, 4-story residential development will include 35 units, with 75 basement and ground floor garage parking spaces to serve the existing Bigelow building and the Project, and will contain 46 indoor resident bike spaces and 10 exterior visitor bike spaces.
