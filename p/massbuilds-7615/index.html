110 Ocean Ave, Revere

56 homes · Under construction · Revere.

Construction of a new 7-story, supportive and affordable housing development located between MBTA Beachmont and MBTA Revere Beach on the Blue Line. The site is immediately adjacent to an MBTA owned pedestrian bridge over the ROW. The building includes 8 group living units, commercial space for the non-profit community service provider, 12 one-bedroom apartments and 36 two and three-bedroom apartments for families. Work also includes demolition of an existing 2-story masonry structure. Primary considerations include the following: Protection of the existing MBTA pedestrian bridge. Protection of the ROW
