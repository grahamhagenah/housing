190 Bowdoin Street

33 homes · Approved · Dorchester, Boston.

Hollins Park will be a 100% affordable new construction project providing 33 units of rental housing in the Greater Bowdoin-Geneva area of Dorchester. Two-thirds of these units will be two- and three-bedrooms to accommodate families. The project will be four stories and include 36 interior bike bike storage spots, eight exterior visitor bike parking spots, eight exterior vehicle parking spaces, and a community room with adjoining roof deck. Hollins Park draws inspiration from the surrounding Dorchester community and will mesh with the neighborhood's architecture. The building will be fully accessible and residents will have elevator access to all floors, including the lobby, management office, community room, and residential units.
