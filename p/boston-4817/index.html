329 Blue Hill Avenue

40 homes · Proposed · Roxbury, Boston.

329 Blue Hill Avenue is a proposed five-story, 40-unit, income-restricted family rental development located along Boston’s Blue Hill Avenue corridor in the Dorchester/Roxbury area of Suffolk County. The project is planned as new construction on an approximately 23,708-square-foot (0.54-acre), currently vacant site that is owned by Caribbean Integration Community Development.
