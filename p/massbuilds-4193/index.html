Quarry Hills II Apartments

269 homes · Complete · West Quincy, Quincy.

269 luxurious apartments located in Quincy just off of Interstate 93, minutes from the Granite Links Golf Club and Downtown Boston. Two new buildings will be built at Quarry Hills II; about half of apartments will be one-bedrooms, and about half will be two-bedrooms. Quarry Hills II is expected to open in Summer 2019 and is located a short drive from the MBTA Red Line and Commuter Rail, as well as Interstate 95/128.
