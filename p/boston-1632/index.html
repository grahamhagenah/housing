181 West First Street

144 homes · Approved · South Boston, Boston.

The NPC Project calls for the development of 144 residential units and garage parking for 100 spaces within the building. The existing two-story warehouse building will be demolished to make space for the new four (4) story building along West Second Street and a six (6) story building along West First Street, and the Project Site will be subject to any necessary remediation.
