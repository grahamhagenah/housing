101 Boston Street

59 homes · Approved · Dorchester, Boston.

The Proponent proposes the revitalization of this underutilized Project Site with the construction of a new six (6) story, multifamily residential building of up to approximately 49,900 square feet of total Gross Floor Area (“GFA”), with up to 59 units of residential housing, associated residential indoor and outdoor amenity spaces, a ground floor restaurant/takeout restaurant and/or retail space of up to approximately 2,211 square feet, and up to ten (10) onsite vehicular garaged parking spaces with access from an existing curb cut along the Frontage Road, along with related improvements in pedestrian accessibility, landscape, and public realm improvements in and around the Project Site, consistent with the character and housing needs of the surrounding community.
