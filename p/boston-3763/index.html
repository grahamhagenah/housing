4198 Washington Street

31 homes · Under construction · Roslindale, Boston.

Auros, LLC, an affiliate of Arx Urban (collectively, the "Proponent"), proposes the redevelopment of the approximately 8,982 square foot site at 4198 - 4206 Washington Street in Roslindale. As contemplated, the Proposed Project calls for the construction of a new five (5)-story, mixed-use building containing approximately thirty-one (31) residential units, approximately 6,800 square feet of retail/community theater space, and approximately thirty-eight (38) bicycle storage spaces.
