Nubian Square Ascends

15 homes · Approved · Roxbury, Boston.

The Nubian Square Ascends Project will transform Nubian Square into a cultural, entertainment, and artistic mecca that celebrates the rich diversity of the neighborhood. This mixed-use project will be developed by an all-minority team. Nubian Square Ascends will offer retail, entertainment, Artist Housing, commercial uses, and a 300-space parking garage.
