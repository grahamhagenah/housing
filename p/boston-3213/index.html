Old Colony Phase Three

305 homes · Complete · South Boston, Boston.

Old Colony Phase Three A includes the construction of up to135 housing units in two new four-story sustainably-designed buildings. Old Colony Phase Three B4 Limited Partnership and Old Colony Phase Three B9 Limited Partnership, the Phase Three B Applicants, seek to construct an approximately 105,000 square foot development comprised of one hundred fifteen (115) one, two, three and four-bedroom residential units in one four-story elevator building, known as Building B, with accessory property management and maintenance space located on the first floor. Old Colony Phase 3C Limited Partnership, the Phase Three C Applicant, seeks to construct an approximately 51,000 square foot development comprised of fifty-five (55) one bedroom units in one four-story elevator building, known as Building C, with first floor resident service space for low-income seniors.
