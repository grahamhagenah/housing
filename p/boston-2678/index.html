Dock Square Garage

125 homes · Approved · Downtown, Boston.

The Proponent proposes to retain the Existing Building and its current parking garage and restaurant-with-pool-tables-and-live entertainment uses, and construct a new, ten-level, approximately 125-unit residential condominium addition on the roof. The dwellings would be served by a new ground-level lobby, facing the Open Space, and new, private open space at the center of the 9th level and open to the sky.
