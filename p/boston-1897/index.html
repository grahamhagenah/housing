Parcel 10 - Madison Tropical

110 homes · Proposed · Roxbury, Boston.

The project is the third and final phase of MPDC's redevelopment of BPDA Parcel 10. Prior phases include the Tropical Foods supermarket (Phase 1, completed in 2015) and the 2101 Washington Street mixed-use building (Phase 2, completed 2017). 2085 Washington (Phase 3) received Article 80 Large Project Review approvals in May 2022 and was granted zoning variances by the Zoning Board of Appeal in May 2023 for a 10-story, 115,366 SF, mixed-income building containing 96 units of housing. The proposed changes include lowering the building height from 10 to 7 stories, reducing the gross building area from 115,366 GSF to 84,296 GSF, and reconfiguring the residential program from 96 mixed-income units (64 rental, 32 homeownership) to 80 affordable rental units.
