59-63 Belgrade Avenue

31 homes · Complete · Roslindale, Boston.

Located at 59-63 Belgrade Ave (two parcels) in Boston’s Roslindale neighborhood, the project contemplates the demolition of a one story funeral home in order to construct a 4-story 31 rental residential building with parking area below containing 23 parking spaces. The proposed project will include fifteen(15) one bedroom units and sixteen (16) two bedroom units.
