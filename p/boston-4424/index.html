65-67 Walnut Street

39 homes · Proposed · Hyde Park, Boston.

The proposed project is to construct a new six (6) story residential building, consisting of thirty-nine (39) residential home ownership units and seventeen (17) vehicular parking spaces. Thirty-nine (39) bicycle parking spaces will be offered internal to the building. The total gross floor area of the Proposed Project will be approximately 49,487 square feet. The unit mix will be composed of twenty (20) one-bed/one-bathroom units and nineteen (19) two-bed/two-bathroom units. The one-bed units will range from 804 to 850 square feet, while the two-bed units will range from 1,180 to 1,225 square feet. All units will have in-unit laundry. Parking will be located in garage space internal to the building at the ground-floor level. The garage will be accessed off of Walnut Street. Trash and recycling will also be contained within the building on the ground floor.
