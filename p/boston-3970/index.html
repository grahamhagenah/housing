16 Taber Street

30 homes · Complete · Roxbury, Boston.

The Proposed Project consists of a six-story (6) building. There will be thirty (30) residential homeownership units on floors two through six. The first floor will contain 2,214 square feet of commercial space, a bicycle storage room for forty-two (42) bicycles, a garage with five (5) parking spaces, and an exercise room.
