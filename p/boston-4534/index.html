1208C VFW Parkway

55 homes · Approved · West Roxbury, Boston.

The Approved Project at 1208C VFW Parkway (also known as 270 Baker Street) in West Roxbury was approved for fifty-five (55) residential rental units, nine (9) of which were to be Inclusionary Development Policy ("IDP") units, and a maximum fifty-five (55) off-street parking spaces.
