Reserved Channel Development

1,050 homes · Proposed · South Boston Waterfront, Boston.

The Project is proposed to include approximately 1,559,000 square feet of development in eight new buildings. Building uses are proposed to include residential housing that responds to the needs of the neighborhood, commercial research laboratory, and ground floor retail. Building massing and heights will be appropriately designed to respect the surrounding neighborhood along West First Street and the residential section of South Boston, with increasing building heights moving north, away from West First Street.
