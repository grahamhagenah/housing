Veterans Home at Chelsea (SLH)

241 homes · Proposed · Chelsea.

Veterans Home at Chelsea - This project is the redevelopment of the Veterans Home Domiciliary Campus on the site of the Veterans Home at Chelsea, which will provide new and modern housing accommodations for both the current veterans living on site and new veterans, creating 241 units. Pennrose will break ground on phase 1 of this project in mid-2026.
