One Chestnut Place

140 homes · Complete · Quincy Center, Quincy.

A new development in the Quincy Center area of the city, featuring 140 units and 4,00 square of retail in close proximity to the MBTA red line. The 15 story structure, the tallest structure in the city, is a part of ongoing process to redevelop Quincy's downtown area. There are no parking spaces on site--residents use a private entry to the municipal parking facility next door.
