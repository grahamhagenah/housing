780 American Legion Highway

114 homes · Proposed · Roslindale, Boston.

The Property consists of approximately 2.58 acres located within the Roslindale neighborhood of Boston. It is currently occupied by the Home for Little Wanderers and contains existing office and community service uses, associated surface parking, circulation areas, and landscaped open space. The Property is mapped within the Two-Family Residential ("2F-5000 ") Subdistrict of the Roslindale Neighborhood District, as well as the Greenbelt Protection Overlay District (GPOD). While the applicable dimensional requirements and required permits will be further detailed in the forthcoming Project Notification Form, it is anticipated at this time that variances will be required for Use, Floor Area Ratio (FAR), Building Height, Usable Open Space, and Minimum Parking.
