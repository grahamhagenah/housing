280-300 Washington Street

255 homes · Approved · Downtown, Boston.

294 Washington Street Owner LLC (an applicant of the City of Boston's Office to Residential Conversion Program) proposes the conversion of an existing 11-story office building located at 294 Washington Street to residential use. Resulting in a new net residential total of 202,000 sqft and approximately 255 new residential rental units. The proposed office-to-residential conversion presents an opportunity to reposition an underutilized downtown commercial building as a vibrant residential address, with ground floor retail, in the heart of Boston’s Financial District.
